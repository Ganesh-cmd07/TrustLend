# TrustLend loan and repayment behavior

Loan applications contain a requested amount, an applicant-selected annual interest rate, and a term from 1 to 120 months. The service does not define a universal advertised APR range; a request can specify 0 to 100 percent. Before applying, check the score-based maximum. The implementation calculates simple interest as principal multiplied by annual rate multiplied by term months, divided by 1200. It divides the total across monthly installments, with rounding remainder applied to the last installment. The first installment is due one month after funding.

Only a lender account can fund an application. Group-backed applications need the group's leader to co-sign first. Funding and repayments move simulated balances between TrustLend wallets and write ledger entries. They are not bank transfers or real payment-provider transactions. A borrower can pay the next unpaid installment when their simulated wallet has enough balance. Pending installments past their due date are marked overdue by the scheduled daily check; the application does not automatically debit wallets.

New applications start SUBMITTED and require an administrator decision. Approval makes an application available to lenders; rejection requires an administrator reason recorded with its reviewer and timestamp. Group-backed applications require the leader co-signature before admin approval. Approved applications move to FUNDED when a lender funds them, ACTIVE after a repayment, and REPAID after all installments are paid. Legacy PENDING applications from the earlier workflow remain fundable during migration. Ask the authenticated account's application endpoint for current personal application data; this published policy document contains no personal account records.
