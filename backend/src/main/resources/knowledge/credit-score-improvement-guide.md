# TrustLend Credit Score Improvement and Tier Guidelines

## Credit Score Overview
TrustLend employs an explainable, transparent rule-based credit scoring engine bounded between 300 and 850 points. Unlike opaque commercial credit bureaus, every factor is directly tied to platform actions and verified financial behavior.

## Score Tiers and Loan Limits
- **300 to 549 Points (HIGH Risk - Below Threshold):**
  - Loan Limit: ₹0.00
  - The borrower cannot submit loan applications until reaching 550.
- **550 to 649 Points (HIGH Risk - Entry Threshold):**
  - Loan Limit: Up to ₹25,000.00
  - Eligible for entry-level micro-loans and community peer funding.
- **650 to 749 Points (MEDIUM Risk):**
  - Loan Limit: Up to ₹50,000.00
  - Eligible for intermediate personal and small enterprise loans.
- **750 to 850 Points (LOW Risk):**
  - Loan Limit: Up to ₹1,00,000.00
  - Premium tier with lowest risk categorization and maximum platform funding limits.

## How to Boost Your Score from 500 to 550 Fast
All new accounts begin at a base score of 500. To quickly unlock the ₹25,000 loan limit:
1. **Join an Active Self-Help Group (+50 Points):**
   - Active membership in any verified SHG immediately adds 50 points to your score.
   - 500 base + 50 SHG = 550 points, instantly unlocking loan eligibility.
2. **Maintain a Healthy Wallet Balance (+25 to +50 Points):**
   - Maintaining at least ₹500 in your TrustLend wallet earns +25 points.
   - Maintaining at least ₹1,000 in your wallet earns +50 points.
3. **Compound with Timely Repayments (+50 Points per Loan):**
   - Every fully repaid loan adds 50 points to your score. Repaying 3 consecutive loans adds +150 points, advancing you from HIGH risk to MEDIUM risk (650+) and LOW risk (750+).

## Factors that Damage Your Score
- **Overdue Loans (-100 Points):**
  - Having even one overdue installment immediately deducts 100 points from your credit score.
  - A borrower with a score of 550 who defaults on an installment will drop to 450, falling back into the ineligible ₹0.00 limit bracket.
