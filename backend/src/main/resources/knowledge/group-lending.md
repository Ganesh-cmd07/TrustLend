# TrustLend self-help groups

A borrower can create a group and becomes its leader. Borrowers may request to join active groups; the leader reviews pending membership requests. Active members can contribute from their simulated TrustLend wallet. Group-backed loan applications require active membership and a leader co-signature before a lender can fund them. A leader cannot co-sign their own group loan or apply for their own group's loan as an ordinary member.

Group savings and contributions are platform records backed by wallet ledger entries. The application does not connect group balances to an external bank account. Membership approval and loan co-signing are separate steps.
